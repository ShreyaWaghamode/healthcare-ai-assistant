PATIENT DISCHARGE INSTRUCTIONS

Purpose:
These instructions help patients safely continue recovery after discharge from the healthcare facility.

Medication Instructions:
Patients should:
- Take medications exactly as prescribed
- Follow dosage schedules carefully
- Avoid skipping doses
- Contact providers regarding side effects

Follow-Up Care:
Patients may need follow-up appointments depending on their condition and treatment plan.

Warning Signs:
Patients should seek immediate medical attention if they experience:
- Difficulty breathing
- Chest pain
- Severe bleeding
- High fever
- Sudden worsening of symptoms

Dietary Recommendations:
Patients should follow dietary instructions provided by healthcare professionals.

Activity Restrictions:
Patients may need to avoid:
- Heavy lifting
- Strenuous exercise
- Driving after certain procedures

Wound Care:
Patients should:
- Keep wounds clean and dry
- Monitor for redness or swelling
- Follow dressing change instructions

Emergency Contact:
For urgent medical concerns, patients should contact emergency services or visit the nearest emergency department.

Telehealth Follow-Up:
Certain follow-up consultations may be completed through telehealth appointments if approved by the provider.
