HIPAA PRIVACY AND SECURITY GUIDELINES

Purpose:
This document describes patient privacy protections and security requirements for handling protected health information (PHI).

Protected Health Information:
PHI includes:
- Patient names
- Medical records
- Insurance information
- Test results
- Billing details
- Appointment history

Access Control:
Only authorized healthcare staff may access patient records necessary for treatment, payment, or healthcare operations.

Data Security:
Healthcare systems must implement:
- Password-protected access
- Multi-factor authentication
- Encrypted data transmission
- Secure backup procedures

Patient Rights:
Patients have the right to:
- Access their medical records
- Request corrections to inaccurate information
- Obtain copies of medical records
- Request restrictions on information sharing

Email and Communication:
Patient information should not be shared through unsecured email systems. Approved secure communication platforms must be used.

Telehealth Privacy:
Telehealth consultations must be conducted through secure, encrypted platforms that comply with HIPAA security requirements.

Incident Reporting:
Potential privacy breaches must be reported immediately to the compliance officer for investigation.

Data Retention:
Medical records must be retained according to federal and state regulatory requirements.
