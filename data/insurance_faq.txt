 INSURANCE ELIGIBILITY FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What insurance plans are accepted?
The healthcare organization accepts most major commercial insurance providers, Medicare, and selected Medicaid plans.

How can patients verify insurance eligibility?
Patients may verify eligibility by:
- Contacting the billing department
- Using the patient portal
- Calling their insurance provider directly

When should insurance information be updated?
Patients should update insurance information whenever:
- Insurance coverage changes
- A new insurance card is issued
- Personal information changes

What happens if insurance is inactive?
If insurance coverage is inactive, patients may:
- Self-pay for services
- Reschedule non-urgent appointments
- Provide updated insurance details

Are telehealth visits covered by insurance?
Many insurance providers cover telehealth visits; however, coverage varies depending on the insurance plan and state regulations.

What documents are required during registration?
Patients may be asked to provide:
- Government-issued photo identification
- Insurance card
- Referral authorization if applicable

What are copayments?
Copayments are fixed amounts patients may need to pay at the time of service depending on their insurance policy.

How are billing disputes handled?
Patients may contact the billing department to review and resolve billing concerns.
