 
TELEHEALTH SERVICES POLICY

What is telehealth?
Telehealth allows patients to consult healthcare providers remotely using video calls, phone calls, or secure online platforms.

Who can use telehealth services?
Patients with non-emergency medical concerns may be eligible for telehealth appointments depending on provider availability and medical condition.

How can patients schedule a telehealth appointment?
Patients may schedule telehealth visits through:
- Patient portal
- Hospital website
- Customer support helpline
- Mobile healthcare application

What devices are required for telehealth visits?
Patients may use:
- Smartphone
- Tablet
- Laptop
- Desktop computer
with internet connectivity, camera, and microphone access.

Are telehealth visits covered by insurance?
Many insurance providers cover telehealth services; however, coverage depends on the patient's insurance plan and state regulations.

Can prescriptions be provided during telehealth visits?
Healthcare providers may prescribe medications during telehealth consultations if medically appropriate and permitted by regulations.

Can patients request medication refills through telehealth?
Yes, patients can request medication refills through telehealth if the medication is already prescribed and does not require an in-person evaluation.

What should patients do before a telehealth appointment?
Patients should:
- Test internet connection
- Ensure device camera and microphone work properly
- Keep identification and insurance documents ready
- Join the session 10 minutes before the appointment

Can telehealth be used for emergency conditions?
No. Patients experiencing emergency medical conditions should immediately contact emergency services or visit the nearest emergency department.

How are telehealth appointment confirmations sent?
Appointment confirmations may be sent through:
- SMS
- Email
- Patient portal notifications

Can telehealth appointments be rescheduled?
Yes, telehealth appointments may be rescheduled based on provider availability.

What happens if a telehealth connection fails?
Patients may:
- Rejoin the session
- Switch to phone consultation if approved
- Contact technical support for assistance

Are telehealth consultations secure?
Yes, healthcare organizations use secure and HIPAA-compliant communication platforms to protect patient information and privacy.

Can specialists provide telehealth consultations?
Yes, selected specialists may offer telehealth consultations depending on medical specialty and patient condition.
