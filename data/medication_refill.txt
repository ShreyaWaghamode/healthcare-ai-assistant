MEDICATION REFILL POLICY

Purpose:
This policy outlines procedures for requesting and approving prescription medication refills.

Eligibility for Refill:
Medication refills may be approved when:
- The medication was previously prescribed by an authorized provider
- The patient has attended required follow-up appointments
- No adverse reactions or contraindications are documented

Methods for Refill Request:
Patients may request refills through:
- Patient portal
- Telephone support line
- Telehealth consultation
- In-person clinic visit

Controlled Substances:
Controlled medications may require:
- In-person evaluation
- Additional identity verification
- Periodic laboratory testing
- Provider approval

Processing Time:
Routine refill requests are generally processed within 48 business hours.

Refill Denial:
Refill requests may be denied if:
- The patient has not completed required follow-up visits
- The medication requires physical assessment
- Safety concerns are identified by the provider

Emergency Refills:
Limited emergency refills may be provided at provider discretion for essential medications.

Pharmacy Coordination:
Approved refill prescriptions are electronically transmitted to the patient's preferred pharmacy.
